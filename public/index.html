From the case

Cupcakes worth the drive

The display case is the daily draw—fresh cupcakes in a rotating mix of flavors, finished by hand and ready for pickup during open hours. Customers come back for the taste and the variety; if you want something specific for an event, call ahead.

“The cupcakes are always so fresh and yummy! Many different flavors to choose from.”

Made to order

Custom cakes, made for the occasion

Birthday cakes, themed celebrations, cookies, and cakes that match the day you are planning. Tell Sweet B's the date, the look, and the flavor—then pick up at the shop on S FM 565.

“We called to see if she could do a last minute wedding cake. And when I say last minute, I mean literally 4 days before hand. She was amazing. Our cake was absolutely gorgeous and it tasted AMAZING as well.”

The shop

Step into the pink room

Two tables, four teal chairs, hanging florals, the hedge wall, and the neon that says you are in the right place. Coffee is set out while you choose from the case. A small bakery with a clear point of view—easy to find once you see the pink door.

The pink room is part of the visit: florals overhead, teal chairs, and a case that puts the baking out front.
